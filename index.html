The short version

Be a Goalkeeper keeps everything on your phone. It has no servers, sends no network requests, shows no ads, contains no analytics, and asks for no account. Nothing you do in the game reaches us or anyone else.

We wrote the long version anyway, because Google Play requires one and because a policy that only says “we respect your privacy” tells you nothing.

What the app stores on your device

All of it lives in the app’s private storage area, which only this app can read.

WhatWhy it exists
The name you choose for your goalkeeperIt is shown in the game and spoken by the commentator
Career progress — chapter, matches played, match history, milestones, rankSo the game continues where you left it
Player statistics — reflexes, diving, positioning, experience, levelThe progression system
Inventory — coins, gems, hearts, gloves, teammates, avatar and frameWhat you own
Settings — music volume, sound volume, language, privacy switchesYour preferences

That is the complete list. There is no hidden field.

What the app does not do

These are statements of fact about the build you are running, not promises about intent:

  • No network access — enforced by Android, not by our good intentions. The app does not request the INTERNET permission. Android therefore refuses it any network connection at the operating-system level: even if a future bug tried to send something, the phone would block it. On top of that, the app contains no HTTP client, no socket, and no websocket code.
  • No advertising. No ad SDK is present and no advertising identifier (AAID) is read, stored, or sent.
  • No analytics or telemetry. No usage statistics, crash reports, or event logs are collected.
  • No account, no sign-in. The app does not ask who you are.
  • No purchases. There is no real-money billing. Coins and gems are earned in the game and have no monetary value.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files. The app requests none of these permissions.
  • No third-party SDKs that process personal data.

Device information

When you open the developer diagnostic screen, the app reads your device model, operating system version, graphics adapter name, screen size, and screen density, and displays them to you on that screen. These values are used to choose the right layout and are never stored and never transmitted. They exist only for as long as the screen is open.

Children

Be a Goalkeeper is not directed at children. The only thing that could identify anyone is the goalkeeper name a player types, and that name stays in the app’s private storage on the phone — we never receive it and cannot read it. Because nothing is transmitted to us or to any third party, and no advertising is shown, we do not process children’s data under GDPR Article 8, COPPA, or Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law (UU 27/2022).

Your rights, and how to exercise them

Because your data never leaves your device, you do not need to ask us for it.

  • Access it: it is in the app, on the screens that show it.
  • Delete it: uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps → Be a Goalkeeper → Storage → Clear data, erases everything permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.
  • Take it elsewhere: the save is a JSON file in the app’s private storage.

We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to correct, export, or delete on your behalf.

Data sharing

We share nothing, because we hold nothing. We do not sell, rent, or disclose personal data. There is no category of recipient to list.

Changes to this policy

This policy describes the app as it is built today. If a future version adds advertising, analytics, accounts, cloud saves, or in-app purchases, this policy will be updated in the same release that adds them, the effective date will change, and the app will tell you inside Settings before the new behaviour begins. We will not quietly widen what we collect.

The features currently planned but not present are listed here so the comparison is honest: advertising (Google AdMob), Google sign-in with cloud saved games, and in-app purchases. None of them is in the app today. Each of them, when it arrives, changes what this document says.
